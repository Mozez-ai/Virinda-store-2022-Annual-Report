# 👗 Virinda Store 2022 Annual Report

Welcome to the **2022 Annual Report Analysis** of **Virinda**, a prominent Indian clothing store! This project was developed using **Microsoft Excel** to summarize, visualize, and interpret the store’s performance data over the full year.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

To create a clean, professional, and insight-driven annual report that helps stakeholders understand:

- Total revenue and monthly performance trends
- Best-selling clothing categories and products
- Regional or branch-wise sales contributions
- Expense breakdown and profit insights
- Customer buying behavior and seasonal peaks

---

## 🧰 Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**  
  - Pivot tables  
  - Charts and dashboards  
  - Conditional formatting  
  - Data cleaning and formula-based calculations

---

## 📈 Key Insights (Sample Highlights)

- 📅 **December** was the highest-grossing month due to holiday sales
- 🧣 **Women's ethnic wear** drove the most revenue across all categories
- 🏬 The **Mumbai branch** outperformed others by 18% on average
- 💰 Profit margins increased steadily in Q3 and Q4
